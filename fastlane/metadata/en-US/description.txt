Total Calories shows the total calories you burn each day, active plus resting, and your step count on your Apple Watch face, iPhone Home Screen and Lock Screen. The watch complications, widgets and daily dashboard are free, with no account, and your health data stays on your iPhone.

Apple Watch's Move goal counts active calories only. Total Calories adds your resting burn from Apple Health, so you see the full number you burn in a day.

ON YOUR APPLE WATCH
• Total calories complications for circular, rectangular, inline and corner slots on any watch face that supports them
• A step count complication
• A watch app with today's totals and the last 30 days
• Goals you set on iPhone sync to the watch

HOME SCREEN AND LOCK SCREEN WIDGETS
• Small, medium and large Home Screen widgets
• Circular and rectangular Lock Screen widgets
• Widgets show the metrics and goals you turned on, and a tap opens the app

TODAY
• Total calories burned on one ring, with steps and a goal bar
• A pace check that compares now with your usual day at this time
• Ask Siri for today's calories or steps
• An optional alert when you reach your daily goal

HISTORY
• 7-day, 30-day, 90-day and 1-year views with bar charts, trend arrows and peak days
• CSV export of your daily data

BMI
• Your BMI from the height and weight already in Apple Health, or entered by hand
• A plain note on what the number does and does not tell you

GOALS
• Optional daily calorie and step goals, or no goals at all
• Pace compared with the same weekday or all days, over the last 14 days, 30 days, 90 days or year

FREE AND VITALS+
Everything above is free and does not expire. Vitals+ is an optional upgrade, as a monthly or yearly subscription, each with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Vitals+ adds:
• Active vs. resting: how much of today's burn came from activity
• Net Deficit: calories burned minus the food energy logged in Apple Health
• Macros: protein, carbs and fat from the food app that already writes to Apple Health, with optional gram goals
• TDEE and BMR: your 30-day maintenance and resting burn averages from Apple Health
• End-of-day projections for calories and steps at your current pace
• Goal streaks and a Sunday recap of this week against last week
• Custom date ranges in History, with summary PDF reports
• Deep Trends: any period compared with the one before it
• Body fat and calorie context next to your BMI
• Excluded days, to keep sick days and days off the wrist out of your averages
• An evening reminder when a goal is still open

PRIVACY
• Apple Health access is read only. The app never writes to your health records
• Your health data is stored on your iPhone and never uploaded
• Reports are generated on your iPhone
• No ads, no tracking and no account
• Purchases are processed by Apple and RevenueCat, which never receive your health data

Prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.
