Total Calories reads calorie and step data from Apple Health (HealthKit) in read-only mode. It does not write to HealthKit. Health data is stored locally on the device and is never uploaded; there is no account and no backend of ours. Subscriptions are processed by Apple and by RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID plus purchase and entitlement status only. RevenueCat never receives HealthKit data.

WHAT CHANGED IN THIS VERSION

1.9.3 adds arrows beside the date on Today to step through past days, a Language row in Settings, and fixes for Weekly Recap and subscription status.

No new permissions or data collection.

HEALTHKIT SETUP - PLEASE GRANT ACCESS WHEN PROMPTED

First launch shows a welcome screen. Tapping Continue triggers the iOS HealthKit read-access prompt, then the app asks you to set optional goals (you may skip this), then it shows the Vitals+ subscription screen, which can be dismissed with the "Get Started" button without purchasing.

Please allow all three read types at the prompt:

- Active Energy Burned
- Basal Energy Burned
- Step Count

These power the main dashboard. Nutrition types (Dietary Energy, Protein, Carbohydrates, Total Fat) are requested separately and only if you turn on the Net Deficit or Macros features in Settings, which read food data another app has already written to Apple Health. Total Calories never writes to HealthKit.

If access is denied or not yet granted, the app shows a banner explaining that Health access is needed (no fake or placeholder health numbers). Users can open Settings or the Health app from that banner.
