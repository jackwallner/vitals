Total Calories reads calorie and step data from Apple Health (HealthKit) in read-only mode. It does not write to HealthKit. Health data is stored locally on the device and is never uploaded; there is no account and no backend of ours. Subscriptions are processed by Apple and by RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID plus purchase and entitlement status only. RevenueCat never receives HealthKit data.

WHAT CHANGED IN THIS VERSION

1.8.7 is a bug-fix release for Excluded Days, the Vitals+ feature added in 1.8.6 (Settings > Averages > Excluded Days). The History tab no longer slows down while Pause Averages is on, and the Excluded Days screen shows the correct dates when the device uses a non-Gregorian calendar (for example Buddhist or Japanese). Anyone can open the Excluded Days screen; without a subscription, tapping a day or the pause switch shows the Vitals+ offer, which can be dismissed with "Not now". No new features, permissions, or data collection.

HEALTHKIT SETUP - PLEASE GRANT ACCESS WHEN PROMPTED

First launch shows a welcome screen. Tapping Continue triggers the iOS HealthKit read-access prompt, then the app asks you to set optional goals (you may skip this), then it shows the Vitals+ subscription screen, which can be dismissed with the "Get Started" button without purchasing.

Please allow all three read types at the prompt:

- Active Energy Burned
- Basal Energy Burned
- Step Count

These power the main dashboard. Nutrition types (Dietary Energy, Protein, Carbohydrates, Total Fat) are requested separately and only if you turn on the Net Deficit or Macros features in Settings, which read food data another app has already written to Apple Health. Total Calories never writes to HealthKit.

If access is denied or not yet granted, the app shows a banner explaining that Health access is needed (no fake or placeholder health numbers). Users can open Settings or the Health app from that banner.

iPHONE FEATURES

- Dashboard with animated calorie ring and step progress bar
- Tap to reveal active vs. resting calorie breakdown
- Pacing indicator: current day vs. configurable rolling average (default: 30 days, same weekday) at the same time of day
- History tab: bar charts for 7D / 30D / 90D / 1Y / custom range
- Trend arrows and peak day highlights
- CSV data export
- Home screen widgets (small, medium) and lock screen widgets (circular, rectangular)
- Settings: goals, display toggles, appearance (system / light / dark)

APPLE WATCH

The watch experience is delivered primarily through watch face complications (circular, rectangular, inline, corner). These show live calorie and step data. The companion watch app provides a today view and serves as the complication container.

PRIVACY

- Read-only HealthKit (never writes)
- Health data is stored on the device and is never uploaded
- No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no accounts
- The only network calls are to Apple and to RevenueCat, for purchase and
  subscription status. Neither receives HealthKit data, and RevenueCat sees only
  an anonymous app user ID with purchase and entitlement status.
- Privacy Policy: https://jackwallner.github.io/vitals/privacy-policy.html

Privacy Policy and Support links are accessible in the iPhone Settings sheet and Apple Watch Help sheet.
