New in Vitals+

• End-of-day projection — see where today's total calories are headed, based on your own recent pace.
• Goal streaks — a chip on the Today screen counting the days in a row you've hit your goal.
• Weekly recap — an optional Sunday-evening summary of your last 7 days versus the week before.

All three are opt-in toggles in Settings and off by default, so the free Today view stays exactly as it was.

Plus the usual under-the-hood fixes to keep your calories and steps accurate across the app, widgets, and Apple Watch.
