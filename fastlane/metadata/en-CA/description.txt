Total Calories puts your daily calories burned (active + resting) and step count right where you need them — your iPhone Home Screen, Lock Screen, and Apple Watch face — for free. No subscription needed for the basics. No account. Your health data never leaves your phone.

If you’ve ever wished your Apple Watch face just showed your total calories burned for the day, this is that app.

ON YOUR APPLE WATCH (FREE)
- Total calories burned complications for every watch face style: circular, rectangular, inline, and corner.
- Live step count complication as well.
- Watch app shows today at a glance plus 30-day trends.
- Goals you set on iPhone sync right to the watch.

ON YOUR HOME SCREEN & LOCK SCREEN (FREE)
- Home Screen widgets in small and medium sizes.
- Lock Screen widgets in circular and rectangular layouts.
- Widgets follow the metrics you’ve enabled and the goals you’ve set.
- View full daily totals without opening the app.

THE DASHBOARD (FREE)
- Total daily calories burned on a single clean ring.
- Daily steps with progress bar and goal.
- Pacing indicator: see if you’re ahead or behind a usual day at this time.
- Pull to refresh. Tap the gear for settings.

BODY PROFILE & BMI (FREE)
- Your BMI, calculated from the height and weight already in Apple Health.
- No Health data? Enter height and weight by hand instead.
- BMI category reference, with a plain-English note on what the number does and doesn’t mean.
- Body fat percentage and calorie context are part of Vitals+.

MACROS (VITALS+)
- Protein, carbs, and fat beside your calories, read from the app that already logs your food to Apple Health.
- Nothing to log twice. If it’s in Health, it’s here.
- Track all three, or only the macro you actually count.
- Optional gram goals, plus macro averages, charts, CSV columns, and PDF reports.

HISTORY (FREE)
- 7 / 30 / 90 / 365-day views plus any custom date range.
- Trend arrows, peak days, and clear bar charts.
- CSV data export.

GOALS THAT WORK FOR YOU
- Optional daily calorie and step goals.
- Or skip goals and use it as a clean counter — your choice.
- Pacing comparison: same weekday vs. all days, with configurable lookback (14 / 30 / 90 days / 1 year).

Vitals+ (OPTIONAL)
A small monthly, yearly, or lifetime upgrade unlocks:
- Macros: protein, carbs, and fat from the food you already log to Apple Health
- TDEE and BMR reference figures: your 30-day maintenance and resting energy averages, straight from Apple Health
- Body fat percentage alongside your BMI
- Active vs. resting calorie breakdown
- Net Deficit: calories burned minus food energy from Apple Health
- Monthly Summary PDFs ready to share
- Custom date-range PDF reports
- Deep Trends: period-over-period comparison cards
A free trial is available to new subscribers.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- Read-only Apple Health access. The app never writes to your health records.
- Your health data is stored on your device and is never uploaded.
- Reports are generated on-device.
- No ads. No tracking. No accounts. No sign-up.
- Subscriptions are handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, our billing provider, which sees only purchase and subscription status. Neither ever receives your health data.

Total Calories is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, or prevent any medical condition. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice.

Subscription Details
Vitals+ is available as an auto-renewing subscription (Monthly or Yearly, both with a 7-day free trial) or as a one-time Lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
