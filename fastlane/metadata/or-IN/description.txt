Apple Watch face ଆଜିର ମୋଟ ଜଳିଥିବା କ୍ୟାଲୋରି ସ୍ପଷ୍ଟ ଦେଖାଇବାକୁ ଚାହିଁଥିଲେ, ଏହା ସେହି app।

APPLE WATCH (ମାଗଣା)
- ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ watch face style ପାଇଁ ମୋଟ ଜଳିଥିବା କ୍ୟାଲୋରି complications: circular, rectangular, inline, corner।
- Live step count complication।
- Watch app ଆଜିର summary ଏବଂ 30‑ଦିନ trends।
- iPhone ରେ set goals ସିଧା watch କୁ sync।

HOME SCREEN & LOCK SCREEN (ମାଗଣା)
- Home Screen widgets ଛୋଟ ଏବଂ medium sizes।
- Lock Screen widgets circular, rectangular layouts।
- Widgets ଆପଣ on କରିଥିବା metrics, goals ଦେଖାଏ।
- App ଖୋଲିବା ବିନା full daily total।

DASHBOARD (ମାଗଣା)
- ଗୋଟିଏ clean ring ରେ daily total calories burned।
- Daily steps, progress bar, goal।
- Pacing indicator: ଏହି ସମୟରେ usual day ଠାରୁ ahead କି behind?
- Pull to refresh. Gear icon tap for settings.

HISTORY (ମାଗଣା)
- 7 / 30 / 90 / 365‑day views, custom date range।
- Trend arrows, peak days, bar charts।
- CSV export।

ଆପଣଙ୍କ ପାଇଁ ଉପଯୁକ୍ତ goals
- Optional daily calorie, step goals।
- Goals skip କରି clean counter ଭାବେ use।
- Pacing: same weekday vs all days, lookback (14 / 30 / 90 days / 1 year)।

Vitals+ (optional)

- Active vs resting calorie breakdown
