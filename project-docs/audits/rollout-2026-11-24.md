## Good moments chosen

1. A daily calorie or step goal crossed while the user has the app open (the celebration toast is the result they just saw).
2. A goal-streak milestone (7, 14, 30, 60, 100 days) reached. Month reviews do not ask: a summary is not a win.

Both are clearly positive results. Weekly recap read and report generated were user actions, not outcomes; widget, complication, tracked days and subscriber tenure were counts or timers. Those were removed.

Gates unchanged: second cold launch, 30-day cooldown stamped when the call is made, opening the write-review page ends the passive asks, feedback never does, never before setup finishes. The ask is Apple's native prompt, no pre-question, never from a button; Settings keeps its Write a Review and Rate App rows. The app has no analytics capture call, so `rating_asked` is not logged.

Trigger audit before the change: goal hit (kept), streak milestone (kept), habit 7 days (removed, count), subscriber 30 days (removed, timer), widget and complication (removed, state at launch), recap read and report generated (removed, user action), pending moment replayed at the first real-data load after launch (removed, launch trigger).

## Frequency follow-up (Rollout R, 2026-11-25)

Result: changed. Rollout S left two moments, a daily goal hit and a streak milestone (7, 14, 30, 60, 100 days). Neither is reached by most users in sessions 2 and 3.

How often a typical user hits each moment (estimates from the defaults and the code; the app has no analytics, so there is no measured rate):

| Moment | Typical user in sessions 2-3 |
|---|---|
| Daily goal hit (defaults 2,500 total kcal, 10,000 steps) | Less than half of sessions. Most adults average well under 10,000 steps, and 2,500 kcal total is above many users' burn. The celebration fires only if the goal is already crossed when the app is open. The first goal hit also goes to the notification-permission card instead of the rating ask. |
| Streak milestone 7/14/30/60/100 | Almost never in sessions 2-3: needs 7 days of goal hits. Kept as an extra. |
| Ahead of usual pace (new) | Roughly half of sessions, more by session 3, for users whose Apple Health already has a few days of data (the pace pill needs 3 to 4 sample days, which Apple Watch and iPhone users usually have on day 1). |

New moment: ahead of usual pace. After a Today refresh, today's steps are at least 500 and 10 percent over "usual by now", or today's total calories at least 100 kcal and 10 percent over it (`ReviewMoment.aheadOfUsualPace`). This is the green "N steps ahead of usual pace" pill the user is looking at, a clearly positive result they just saw. Rules:

- Once per local day (`reviewPrompt.paceMomentDay`), counted only when an ask could follow (finished setup, second launch, cooldown clear, write-review page not opened), so a first-session day does not use up the moment.
- Only when pacing is on and visible, Today (not a past day) is shown, no goal toast or dashboard card is up, and the day has data.
- It posts the same notification as a goal hit, so every gate is unchanged: second cold launch, 30-day cooldown stamped when the call is made, 3 second wait then the "Today clear, nothing presented" re-check, a blocked moment is skipped not replayed, one ask per session. No question first, never from a button.
- A goal hit and the pace moment in the same refresh produce one ask (the pace moment skips while the goal toast is up, and one ask per session).

Not logged as `rating_asked`: the app has no analytics capture call.

Files: `Shared/Services/ReviewPromptTracker.swift`, `Vitals/Views/DashboardView.swift`, `VitalsTests/ReviewPromptTrackerTests.swift`, `AGENTS.md`.

Tests added (`ReviewPromptTrackerTests`): ahead on steps, ahead on calories, behind or level is not a moment, a tiny lead (absolute floor and 10 percent share) is not, missing or zero pace data is not, once per day, every gate holds (first launch blocked, setup, cooldown, a blocked first session leaves the day's moment), write-review page stops it.

Held for Jack: the thresholds (500 steps, 100 kcal, 10 percent) are a judgment call; raise them if asks feel early. If sessions 2-3 still need more reach after real data, the next candidate is a lower-tier streak (2 or 3 days).
