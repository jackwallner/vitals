## What survived, ranked

1. CP-1 (CONFIRMED). Weekly Recap reads on and never schedules after a declined permission is later granted. Paying users, permanent, silent, and the app's own recovery button leads into it.
2. SM-1 (CONFIRMED-MINOR). Pause start slips one day earlier after a westward move; the extra day is frozen into the picked list on Resume.
3. SM-2 (CONFIRMED-MINOR). Lower streak tier celebrated after a higher one for users who skip a week of opens.
4. CP-3 (CONFIRMED-MINOR). Custom History range stays selected and visible after a lapse.
5. CP-4 (CONFIRMED-MINOR). Approved Ask to Buy purchases leave no funnel record (analytics only).
6. SM-3 (CONFIRMED-MINOR). Subscriber tenure counts a lapsed stretch; one early rating moment, near-zero marginal effect.
7. CP-2 (CONFIRMED-MINOR). Old recap notification opens the recap for a lapsed user; the audit's fix would not close its own main route.

The audit's order is close. Changes: SM-2 moves above CP-3 and CP-2 drops from fourth to last, because its main route stays open under the proposed guard and the cost is a free look at the user's own data. Fix group 1 in the audit should not treat CP-2 as part of the same change as CP-1.

Missed bugs: none serious found within the audit's scope. One addition to CP-1, recorded in its row: the recap setter does not refresh `notificationStatus`, so the denied footer and the "Turn On in iOS Settings" button are missing right after "Don't Allow".

## Method changes that would have prevented the errors

- Check every stated cost against the platform. PC-2's "re-answer the HealthKit prompts" fails on how HealthKit authorization works.
- Trace a claimed side effect to the condition that gates it. PC-1's notification card is gated on the very flag that a push requires to be on.
- Read the doc comment at the change site before calling something a bug, and cite the comment that is actually broken. PC-1's one-way dedupe is documented at `DailyNudgeService.swift:19-20`; CP-4's cited comment is about the pending case, while the broken one is in `ConversionDiagnostics`.
- Test a proposed fix against the claimed route. CP-2's `isPro` guard reads the cached value on the cold launch the route describes.
- Count the cost relative to the correct path, not in isolation. SM-2's "second upsell sheet and rating moment" also happen for a user who opens daily; only the order is wrong. SM-3's extra moment is swamped by daily goal hits.
- Label severity separately from reachability. Six of seven survivors are minor; a ranked table without a severity column read as seven comparable bugs.
