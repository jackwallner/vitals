Impact

B5

After a move or trip to a time zone further west, the goal streak drops to zero and excluded days land on the wrong day

mediumconfirmeddata
Impact

After moving or travelling west, every cached day written before the move reads as the day before. The goal streak (Today badge, evening reminder, the 7/14/30/60/100 day celebration) collapses to the days since the move, and stays collapsed for the 400 day window if the move is permanent. Excluded days are applied to the wrong day in the cached TDEE/BMR average (Maintenance widget). Watch history merge and the Today past-day cached lookup also use the stale date. Moving east is unaffected.

How it happens
  1. Use the app in New York for a while with a goal set and a 10 day goal streak (Vitals+ streak badge on, or the evening reminder on).
  2. Move or travel to Los Angeles (or any zone behind the old one) and open the app.
  3. Today shows no streak badge (0 days) instead of 10. The evening reminder drops its 'Keep your 10-day streak' title.
  4. Excluded Days: exclude a day, then open the Maintenance widget or any figure built from the cache. The excluded day is still counted and the day after it is dropped instead.
  5. On Apple Watch, merged history puts cached values on the wrong day.
Where
Shared/Services/HealthKitService.swift:1055Shared/Services/HealthKitService.swift:1013Shared/Utilities/MilestoneCalculator.swift:62Shared/Utilities/EnergyAveragesCalculator.swift:34Vitals/Views/DashboardView.swift:2315Vitals/Views/DashboardView.swift:1082Vitals/Utilities/DailyNudgeService.swift:133Shared/Services/HealthKitService.swift:521
Suggested fix

Key everything on dateString, or rewrite existing.date to the current zone's start of day whenever a row is updated (saveHistoryToCache, refreshCache) and on a significant time change. In readers, rebuild the date from the key with ExcludedDays.date(fromKey:) instead of trusting row.date.

Evidence

    existing.restingCalories = day.resting
    existing.steps = day.steps
    existing.lastUpdated = .now      // date is never rewritten

MilestoneCalculator.swift:62:

DailyHealthRecord.date is set once at creation to local midnight in the zone of that moment. The unique key (dateString) is zone free, but saveHistoryToCache and refreshCache update an existing row without rewriting date. fetchCachedHistory returns the stored date. Streak, TDEE, watch merge and past-day lookup then re-bucket that instant with the new zone's calendar (startOfDay or key(for:)). A row made at New York midnight is 21:00 the previous evening in Los Angeles, so it lands on the previous day, and the boundary day goes missing, which ends the streak walk at the move. mediumplausiblenotifications
Impact

Goal Reached pushes and widget numbers can arrive late or only when the app is opened, the main reason a user turns these on.

How it happens
