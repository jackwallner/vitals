# Localization ASO upgrade, round 2, batch r2-2 (2026-11-20)

Locales rewritten: da, sv, no, fi, cs, sk, hu. Nothing was pushed to App Store Connect; edits are in `fastlane/metadata/<locale>/`.

Method: Apple search hints (`MZSearchHints`) per storefront (DK 143458, SE 143456, NO 143457, FI 143447, CZ 143489, SK 143496, HU 143482), iTunes Search API for competitor titles and our rank (trackId 6761743504, top 50 only), indexing table from Apple (https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-store-localizations). Competitor subtitle pages on apps.apple.com return a script-only shell to curl (about 1 KB), so competitor evidence is titles and rating counts only.

## Cross-locale findings

- Indexing (Apple table): each of these seven storefronts indexes its own language plus English (U.K.), and nothing else. None of them indexes en-US. So each locale's keyword field serves one country only, and the English terms (tdee, bmr, apple, watch, calculator, pedometer and so on) are already covered by the en-GB keyword field. Old keyword fields repeated those English words; the new ones carry local-language words. `kcal` and `bmi` stay on purpose: autocomplete shows them next to local words (`kcal tæller`, `räkna kcal`, `kcal számláló`, `pocitani kcal`, `bmi beregner`, `bmi-beräknare`, `bmi kalkulator`), and Apple combines words only inside one locale.
- Burn phrases have no autocomplete in any of the seven storefronts. Every burn seed returned nothing: `forbrændte kalorier`, `förbrända kalorier`, `forbrente kalorier`, `kulutetut kalorit`, `spálené kalorie`, `spálené kalórie`, `elégetett kalória`, and the matching `kalorieforbrug`, `kaloriförbrukning`, `kaloriforbruk`, `kalorinkulutus`, `kalorický výdej`, `elégetett kalória`, `kalóriaégetés`. The one partial hit is Swedish `kaloriförbrukning` -> `kaloriförbrukning för löpning`. So these burn names rest on thin evidence. What supports them: the competing field for the burn phrases is nearly empty (1 to 7 results each, mostly 0-rating utilities), while the head terms (`kalorietæller`, `kaloriräknare`, `kalorilaskuri`, `kalóriaszámláló`) belong to Lifesum (up to 70k ratings in SE), Yazio, MyFitnessPal and fatsecret.
- Naming approach (Jack approved in round 1): every locale below leads the name with the burn phrase and keeps the food-logger head term in the keywords. This is a burn phrase chosen over the head term in all seven locales. Where it matters most: **sv** (old name was the head term `Kaloriräknare`; SE is also a proceeds and downloads market) and **no** (we currently rank 1 for `forbrente kalorier`, and the new name contains that exact phrase, so it should hold).
- Pattern across the three Scandinavian locales: name `<burn phrase> og skridt`, subtitle `Aktiv + hvile, <watch> & widget`. Czech, Slovak and Hungarian use `<burned calories> + steps` in the name and a plain descriptor in the subtitle.
- Brand: none of the names carries "Total Calories", same as the old names. The description opens with `Total Calories` in the first sentence.
- Names checked with the iTunes Search API per storefront (limit 50): no other app has exactly the chosen trackName in DK, SE, NO, FI, CZ, SK, HU. Result counts for the exact names: 2, 2, 2, 0, 3, 1, 0.
- Native review by a human was not done. Highest-risk copy is flagged under "Open points".
- Round 1 review mistakes avoided: one address form per locale (da/sv/no/fi/hu informal, cs/sk formal lowercase), Apple's own UI words used (Sundhed, Hälsa, Helse, Terveys, Zdraví, Zdravie, Egészség; Apple-ID spellings per language), no calques of "food log" (replaced with `Appen logger ikke din mad`, `ingen matdagbok`, `Ruokaa ei kirjata`, `Jídlo se nezapisuje`, `Étkezést nem naplóz`), Slovak spelling checked against Czech (`krokomer`, `výdaj`, `zadarmo`, `zdarma` are Slovak; the old sk keywords had Czech `krokoměr`, `pokoj`).

## Open points for review

- Every name here replaces a head-term-adjacent or literal name with a burn phrase that has no autocomplete evidence in its storefront. The reason is the empty competition and the 1.4.1-safe honesty of describing what the app does, but a decision rests on thin evidence. sv is the one to watch (old name was the head term, SE has proceeds and downloads). Revert path: `git show HEAD:fastlane/metadata/<locale>/name.txt`.
- No human native review. Highest-risk copy: hu (`Napi égetés számlapon, widget` is telegraphic; `Mozgás célja` for the Move goal), fi (`koti-valikko`, `Liiku-tavoite` follow Apple's wording from memory and were not checked against a device), cs and sk formal `vy` (a developer may prefer the informal form), no (`Beveg-målet`, `matdagbok`).
- Autocomplete for these small storefronts is sparse, so the keyword choices beyond the head terms follow the language's own standard words, not suggestions.
