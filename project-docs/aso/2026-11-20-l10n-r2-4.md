# Localization ASO upgrade, round 2, batch r2-4 (2026-11-20)

Seven locales rewritten: ko, th, vi, id, ms, ar-SA, he. Nothing was pushed to App Store Connect; edits are in `fastlane/metadata/<locale>/` (name, subtitle, keywords, promotional text, description). `asc` is not installed on this machine, so `asc migrate validate` was not run.

Method: Apple search hints (`MZSearchHints`) per storefront, iTunes Search API for competitor titles and our rank (trackId 6761743504, top 50 only, so "not in top N" means outside the returned list), Apple's indexing table (https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-store-localizations). Competitor subtitles were not read (apps.apple.com returns a script-only shell to curl in round 1), so competitor evidence is titles and rating counts. Ranks are for the OLD fields, measured before this rewrite.

## Indexing (Apple table, fetched 2026-11-20)

- KR indexes ko (primary) and en-GB. TH, VN, ID, MY, IL and SA index en-GB as primary and the local language (th, vi, id, ms, he, ar) as secondary. US also indexes ar-SA, ko and vi as secondary locales.
- Consequence: in all seven storefronts the en-GB keyword field (tdee, counter, calculator, apple, burned, bmr, deficit, resting, active, pedometer, steps, ring, bmi, intake, move) already supplies the English terms. These locale keyword fields therefore carry local-language terms only, no tdee, bmr, bmi, kcal or apple.

## Naming decision for Jack (burn phrase chosen over the food-logger head term in every locale)

The head term is the food-logger word in each language (칼로리 계산기 / 카운터, hitung kalori, kira kalori, عداد السعرات الحرارية, ספירת קלוריות, tính calo, นับแคลอรี่). Those results are dominated by Yazio, MyFitnessPal, fatsecret and local food loggers with up to tens of thousands of ratings. Each name now leads with the burn phrase and the head term is kept in the keywords so combined phrases still index.

| Locale | New name | Burn phrase | Head term kept in keywords |
|---|---|---|---|
| ko | 총 소모 칼로리 - 일일 추적기 | 소모 칼로리 | 계산기, 카운터, 기록, 측정 (combine with the name's 칼로리) |
| th | แคลอรี่ที่เผาผลาญ - รายวัน | เผาผลาญ | นับแคลอรี่, คำนวณแคลอรี่ |
