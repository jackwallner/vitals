# Localization ASO upgrade, round 2 batch r2-1, 2026-11-20

Locales rewritten: en-AU, en-CA, es-MX, fr-CA, nl-NL, pt-BR, pt-PT, ca. Nothing was pushed to App Store Connect. All edits are in `fastlane/metadata/<locale>/` (name, subtitle, keywords, promotional_text, description only).

Method: Apple search hints (`MZSearchHints`) per storefront, iTunes Search API for competitor titles, our rank (trackId 6761743504, top 50 only) and exact-name collision checks, Apple's indexing table (https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-store-localizations). Competitor subtitle pages are script-only to curl, so competitor evidence is titles and rating counts. Round 1 review mistakes avoided: one address form per locale (tu/vous/je consistent), no calques of "food log", EU/regional terms per market (pt-PT `ecrã`, `défice`, `pedómetro`, `Mexer`; es-MX `carátula`, `dispositivo médico`), no keyword repeats (checked by `scripts/check-locale-fields.py`).

Descriptions: rewritten from the current en-US text (large widgets, Siri, goal alert, BMI, excluded days, evening reminder included). The disclaimer keeps the 1.4.1 wording (not a medical device, no diagnose/treat/prevent claim). The app has no food logging and no kilojoule display (checked `Shared/`): intake is always described as coming from Apple Health, and no kJ keyword was added for AU.

## Naming choices to look at

Burn phrase chosen over the food-logger head term (Jack approved this approach in round 1). In every locale below the old name led with the head term and the head term now sits in keywords or is not indexed in that locale:

| Locale | Old name | New name | Head term now |
|---|---|---|---|
| en-AU | Total Calories - Daily Tracker | Total Calories Burnt Tracker | brand kept, `burnt` added |
| en-CA | Total Calories - Daily Tracker | Total Calories Burned Tracker | brand kept, `burned` added |
| es-MX | Contador de Calorías - Diario | Total de Calorías Quemadas | `contador` in keywords |

| nl-NL | Calorieënteller - Dagelijks | Verbrande Calorieën - Tracker | `calorieënteller` in keywords |

| ca | Total Calories - Diari | Calories Cremades - Diari | none (no demand evidence) |

All eight names were checked for an exact trackName match by another app in the storefronts that index them (AU, NZ, CA, MX, US, NL, BE, BR, PT, ES): none.

Found while checking: round 1's es-ES name `Gasto Calórico Diario` exactly matches another live app named `Gasto calórico diario` (trackId 1151120599) in ES and MX (and the same app shows in BR and PT). App Store Connect refuses a duplicate localized name, so es-ES needs another name before it is pushed. I did not touch es-ES.
