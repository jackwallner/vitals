Reviewer pass over `fastlane/metadata/{ro,hr,sl-SI,el,ru,tr}/` against en-US and the writer's note ([2026-11-20-l10n-r2-3.md](2026-11-20-l10n-r2-3.md)). Claim check: the app has no food logging (read-only HealthKit). No copy in the six locales claims meal logging; intake always appears as energy "recorded in Health" by another app. No competitor names, no emojis, no em dashes.
