# Localization ASO upgrade, round 2, batch r2-3 (ro, hr, sl-SI, el, ru, tr)

Nothing was pushed to App Store Connect. Edits are in `fastlane/metadata/{ro,hr,sl-SI,el,ru,tr}/` only. Method as in round 1 ([2026-11-20-l10n.md](2026-11-20-l10n.md)): Apple `MZSearchHints` per storefront, iTunes Search API for competitor titles, ranks and exact-name checks (our trackId 6761743504), Apple's indexing table fetched 2026-11-20. Competitor subtitle pages are not readable by curl (script-only shell), so competitor evidence is titles and rating counts. Ranks come from the top 50 the API returns, so `none` means outside that list. The Search API shows the live (old) metadata, so our ranks describe the old names.

## Naming decision for Jack (burn phrase over the food-logger head term)

Every locale in this batch changed its name from a translation of the brand ("Total Calories") or the logger head term to the burn phrase. All six old names were literal translations that nobody types. The head term stays in the keyword field in each.

| Locale | Old name | New name | Head term kept in keywords |
|---|---|---|---|
| ro | Total Calorii - Zilnic | Calorii Arse - Tracker Zilnic | contor, calculator |
| hr | Ukupne Kalorije - Dnevni | Potrošnja Kalorija - Praćenje | brojač, kalkulator |
| sl-SI | Skupne Kalorije - Dnevno | Poraba Kalorij - Sledilnik | števec, kalkulator, kalorije |
| el | Σύνολο Θερμίδων - Ημερήσιο | Καύση Θερμίδων - Ημερήσια | θερμίδες, μετρητής, θερμιδομετρητής |
| ru | Счетчик Калорий - Ежедневно | Сожженные Калории - Трекер | счетчик, калорий, калькулятор, подсчет |
| tr | Kalori Sayar - Günlük Takip | Yakılan Kalori - Günlük Takip | sayar, sayacı, hesaplama |

The two that matter most: ru and tr. Their old names (`Счетчик Калорий`, `Kalori Sayar`) are the exact head terms of Yazio, fatsecret and MyFitnessPal (tens of thousands of ratings), and our app has no food logging. ru: old name ranked 2 of 3 for `сожженные калории` (the only 3 apps there) and none for `счетчик калорий`. tr: old name ranked none for `kalori sayacı` and `adım ve kalori sayar`, and 34 of 42 for `yakılan kalori`, where the new name now sits.

## For the reviewer

Their keywords are the evidenced head terms plus plain local words for the TDEE, BMR and deficit rows. A native read is the main remaining risk, especially: sl `Koraki in skupno na zapestju`, hr `Ukupno i koraci na satu`, el `Σερί στόχων` (streaks) and `επιπλοκές` (complications), ro `Calorii Arse - Tracker Zilnic`.

- hr and sl-SI describe the BMI feature with the local acronym (`ITM` in sl, `BMI` in hr, `ΔΜΣ (BMI)` in el); each heading mentions BMI where the local acronym differs.
