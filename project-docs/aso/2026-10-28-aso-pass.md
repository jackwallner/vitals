# App Store ASO review, 2026-10-28

**Verdict: FAIL.** The prior proposal cannot be independently verified until Apple search access returns. The localized descriptions still need native copy review and some English feature coverage. Do not submit these fields as a completed ASO pass.

The first-pass research and prior ranks are [historical](2026-10-28-first-pass-historical.md); they are not independently verified here. Search terms removed by the proposal were restored, as were changed titles and subtitles. Japanese and Simplified Chinese keyword copies of terms still present in their subtitles were removed to satisfy the collision rule. Korean, Thai, Vietnamese and Traditional Chinese duplicate keyword tokens were similarly removed; the same terms remain in their subtitles. This preserves the terms in each locale, although Apple tokenization of compact scripts remains a risk.

Apple search returned HTTP 403 repeatedly over the requested 90-minute retry window. Limited later responses returned US, UK, Australian and Canadian `total calories` at #1. German `verbrannt`, French `besoin calorique`, Spanish `reloj calorías`, Polish `suma kalorii`, and Swedish `förbrända` returned HTTP 403 after cooldowns. Evidence for the proposal, all realistic combinations of removed terms, and the requested per-locale top-10 protections remain unverified. The restoration is precautionary, not a claim that the original rankings are proven or that the proposals would lose them. Thai, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese each have 11 unused keyword characters after duplicate removal. No unsupported replacement was invented.

US supplementary fields still repeat English or international tokens including bmr, bmi and healthkit across locales. They do not form distinct, complete researched English query groups. These pre-existing fields were preserved because their local-language rankings have not been checked. The first-pass Russian calories,burned swap also failed to form the cited total calories burned query within Russian metadata, and has been reverted.

The prior report incorrectly asserted description parity. The free BMI section and Vitals+ TDEE/BMR references, body-fat percentage and evening reminder were missing in de-DE, fr-FR, fr-CA, es-ES, es-MX, ja, pl, it, nl-NL, zh-Hans, pt-BR, sv, ar-SA and ru; this review added them and shortened repeated copy in some fields to fit. English GB, AU and CA descriptions were brought closer to US feature coverage. Some other English features remain omitted in non-English fields, including large widgets, Siri, the goal alert and iPhone Duo. Native-language quality and remaining description parity require independent review.
