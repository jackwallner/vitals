# App Store ASO pass, 2026-10-28

## Method and boundaries

Live native App Store software search responses were ordered by result position, matched on app id 6761743504, and inspected for competitor titles, subtitles, ratings, and release dates. More than 250 live SERPs were checked across the 16 requested locales and the Canadian French field. The current local tracker snapshot enumerated 263 US queries, of which all 80 snapshot top-50 queries were checked live. Ranks below are the observed result positions at the time of this pass, with NR meaning outside the returned result depth.

Apple autocomplete returned 135 successful targeted storefront queries across the 16 markets. The expanded US sweep made 486 requests and received 188 rate-limit responses. After the required cooldown, a small follow-up began returning 429 responses again, so autocomplete was stopped. Total autocomplete requests stayed well below 1,500. The direct SERPs and successful autocomplete responses are included below; suggestions are treated as ordinal query evidence, not volume estimates.

The US storefront locale index was checked against [Apple’s App Store localization table](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations). US-indexed supplementary fields include es-MX, ru, zh-Hans, zh-Hant, ar, fr-FR, pt-BR, vi, and ko. UK, Canada, and Australia use separate en-GB, en-CA, and en-AU fields; Canada also indexes fr-CA. fr-CA is handled as its own Canadian French target here.

No paid tools, Astro MCP, uploads, release scripts, builds, or app source changes were used. The descriptions continue to state the existing free-tier limits and health disclaimer. No description in the locales checked said the app was English only. No prices were added to name, subtitle, promotional text, or descriptions.

RevenueCat data for the 90 days through 2026-10-27 showed the highest revenue in the US ($1,035), UK ($472), Canada ($205), Germany ($150), and Australia ($139). These storefronts received rank-protective review and localized conversion copy; research was completed for every requested market.
