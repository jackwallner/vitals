# Localization ASO upgrade, 2026-11-20

Ten locales rewritten from storefront research. Nothing was pushed to App Store Connect; all edits are in `fastlane/metadata/<locale>/`.

Method: Apple search hints (`MZSearchHints`) per storefront, iTunes Search API for competitor titles and our rank (trackId 6761743504), indexing table from Apple (https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-store-localizations). Competitor subtitle pages on apps.apple.com returned a script-only shell to curl, so competitor evidence is titles and rating counts only. Ranks are from the iTunes Search API (top 50), so `not in top N` means outside the top N returned, not unranked everywhere.

## Cross-locale findings

- en-GB is indexed by GB (primary) and by DE, CH, AT, FR, ES, UA, HK, TW, CN, IT and PL (secondary). None of those storefronts index en-US, so the en-GB keyword field is the only English coverage there. It therefore carries the full high-volume English set (counter, calculator, apple, pedometer, tdee, bmr) rather than only terms missing from en-US.
- ja also gets en-US (Apple table), and US gets zh-Hans, zh-Hant and fr-FR as secondary locales, so those keyword fields serve US searchers as well as the local storefront.
- Food-logger words (Kalorienzähler, Compteur Calories, Contacalorie, Suma Kalorii) pull in Yazio, Fddb, Lifesum and similar apps with tens of thousands of ratings. The app shows calories burned and has no food logging, so DE, FR, ES, IT, PL, UA and JA names now lead with the burn/consumption phrase that autocomplete shows and where the field is thin. The head terms stay in keywords.
- zh-Hans and zh-Hant names were kept: both hold rank 2 for `每日消耗卡路里`.
- Keyword fields do not repeat name or subtitle words (checked by `scripts/check-locale-fields.py`). Some English tokens (tdee, bmr, bmi, apple, watch, kcal) repeat across locales on purpose because the autocomplete evidence shows they are typed in English in every storefront.

- Description: rewritten body in every locale from the current en-US text (large widgets, Siri, goal alert, BMI, excluded days, evening reminder included). Privacy, disclaimer, subscription and link paragraphs are kept from the previous files. The disclaimer keeps the 1.4.1 wording (not a medical device, no diagnose/treat/prevent claims).
