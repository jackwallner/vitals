## Total Calories Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Total Calories reads selected Apple Health data to display calories,
steps, trends, and optional body-profile context. It does not require an
account.

## HealthKit data

With your permission, the app may read active energy burned, basal
energy burned, and step count. If you use body-profile or dietary
features, it may also request height, body mass, body-fat percentage,
and dietary energy. HealthKit data is processed on-device, is not
uploaded, and is not used for advertising, marketing, data mining, or
cross-app tracking. The app does not write to HealthKit.

Daily summaries, history, goals, preferences, widgets, watch data, and
CSV exports are stored or generated locally. Exported files are shared
only through the destination you choose. Revoke HealthKit access in iOS
Settings.

## Purchases

Vitals+ includes monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions and a
one-time lifetime purchase. Apple processes payment. RevenueCat verifies
entitlement status and may process an anonymous app user ID, purchase
and entitlement information, and limited technical app or device
context, including subscription analytics. It does not receive HealthKit
data, health summaries, or CSV exports. See
[RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## Ad attribution

If you install Total Calories from an Apple Ads search ad, Apple's
AdServices framework tells RevenueCat which campaign and keyword led to
the install. This uses no advertising identifier and does not track you
across other apps. We use it only to measure whether our ads pay for
themselves.

## Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it or remove the app. Revoke
HealthKit permission in iOS Settings. Apple and RevenueCat retain
transaction records under their policies. We do not operate a
server-side health database for the app.

## Children and changes

Total Calories is not directed to children under 13 and does not
knowingly collect personal information from children. We may update this
policy when the app or its services change.
