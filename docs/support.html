Total Calories Support

Last updated: May 10, 2026

Total Calories is a personal health tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch that displays your daily calories burned and step count using Apple Health data.

Support contact
Email: jackwallner@gmail.com

What Total Calories Needs

Total Calories requires read-only Apple Health access to:

  • Active Energy Burned
  • Basal Energy Burned
  • Step Count

Troubleshooting

  • If Total Calories shows no data, open the Health app and confirm Total Calories has permission to read calories and steps.
  • If widgets or complications look stale, open Total Calories once to refresh the local cache.
  • If export fails, try again and make sure the destination app or share target is available.

Privacy

Total Calories does not create an account, does not send your data to a server, and does not include analytics, ads, or tracking.

Read the full Privacy Policy.

Additional Help
