## Total Calories for Android Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Total Calories: Daily Tracker (published on Google Play by E3 Apps) reads calories burned and steps from Health Connect and shows them as a daily ring, history, and trends on your Android phone. It does not require an account.

## Health Connect data

With your permission, the app reads active calories burned, total calories burned, basal metabolic rate, and steps. If you turn on Net Deficit, it also reads the food energy you log in Health Connect (nutrition). You can also allow access to history older than 30 days and to background reads so the widget stays fresh; both are optional. The app only reads. It never writes to Health Connect.

This data is processed on your phone to draw the ring, charts, averages, and the reports you create. It is not uploaded to any server, is not sold, is not shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising, marketing, or data mining. The app's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the [Health Connect Permissions policy](https://developer.android.com/health-and-fitness/guides/health-connect/develop/health-connect-policy), including its Limited Use requirements. You can change or revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings > Health Connect > App permissions > Total Calories.

## Data stored on your device

Your goals, display settings, excluded days, and the latest numbers shown by the home-screen widget are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. They are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. A PDF or CSV report is created on your phone and is shared only with the app you pick in the share sheet.

## Purchases

Vitals+ is offered through monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase. Google Play processes payment. RevenueCat verifies purchase status and may process an anonymous app user ID, purchase and entitlement information, simple counts of when the upgrade screen was shown (for example, how many times and on which screen), and limited technical context such as app version and device type. It never receives Health Connect data, health summaries, or reports. See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Ratings

The app may ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review card. Google handles that card; the app does not see your answer.

## What we do not collect

No account, advertising, ad identifiers, analytics SDKs, cross-app tracking, location, contacts, or photos. We do not sell or share personal information.

## Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your phone until you delete it or uninstall the app, which removes it. Health Connect keeps your health records under your control; Total Calories does not hold a copy off your phone. Google and RevenueCat keep transaction records as needed to process and restore purchases.

**To delete your data from Total Calories: Daily Tracker (E3 Apps):**

1. Goals, settings, excluded days, and cached widget numbers: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings > Apps > Total Calories > Storage > Clear storage. None of this is stored off your phone.
2. Health Connect records and permissions: manage them in Android Settings > Health Connect. Total Calories never copies them to a server.
3. Data held by RevenueCat for your anonymous purchase ID (purchase history and upgrade-screen counts): email [jackwallner+tc@gmail.com](mailto:jackwallner+tc@gmail.com?subject=Total%20Calories%20Android%20data%20deletion) with the subject "Total Calories Android data deletion". We delete it within 30 days. Google Play order records are kept by Google under its own policy and are not deleted by this request.

## Health information

Total Calories shows general fitness information from your own data. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, prevent, treat, or cure any condition.

## Children and changes

Total Calories is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We may update this policy when the app or its services change.
