# c521 — UX Improvement Suggestions

_Analysis of the Vitals / Total Calories codebase as of May 2026. Every item is framed from how someone actually uses the app—what they’re trying to do, what they feel today, and what would make that moment clearer or more satisfying. No implementation changes; suggestions only._

---

## How people experience the app today

Most users fall into a few recurring modes:

| Mode | Typical behavior | Primary surfaces |
|------|------------------|------------------|
| **Glance** | Check calories/steps between activities; maybe widget or watch | Today, widgets, watch |
| **Track progress** | See if they’re on pace for goals; celebrate hitting target | Today ring, pacing pills, haptics |
| **Understand trends** | Compare this week to last; export for coach or spreadsheet | History, Deep Trends, CSV/PDF |
| **Configure once** | Set goals, hide metrics, enable Net Deficit, theme | Onboarding, Settings |
| **Upgrade (subset)** | Trial, paywall, Vitals+ tab | Trial sheet, Upgrade tab, locked toggles |

The product is strong on data plumbing (HealthKit → cache → widgets/watch) and visual polish. The biggest UX gaps are **discoverability** (features exist but aren’t obvious), **mental-model alignment** (terms like “net deficit” and “pacing”), and **cross-surface consistency** (phone vs watch vs widget vs third tab).

## 1. First launch and onboarding

### 1.1 Health permission timing feels like a second interruption

**User story:** Someone installs the app, reads the welcome screen, taps Continue, sets goals, taps Get Started—and expects to see their numbers.

**Current experience:** Health authorization is requested when moving from welcome → goals (`Continue`), but the dashboard only refreshes after onboarding dismisses on `Get Started`. If auth is slow or the user hesitates on the system sheet, they land on Today with a spinner or empty state, then may see another permission-related banner.

**Suggestion:** Treat “permissions + first data load” as one explicit onboarding step—e.g. a short “Connecting to Apple Health…” screen that doesn’t dismiss until auth completes *or* fails with a clear next action. Copy should say: “We’ll show your totals as soon as Health responds.”

**Why it helps:** Removes the “I finished setup, why is the app still asking?” feeling and aligns user expectation with system dialogs.

---

### 1.2 Turning off both goals doesn’t explain what Today will look like

**User story:** A user wants a simple counter, so they disable both calorie and step goals in onboarding.

**Current experience:** Goals become `nil` (minimal/counter mode), but `showCalories` / `showSteps` stay on by default—they still see calories and steps, just without rings/goals. That’s often correct, but users who expected “counter only” may not understand why pacing or goal language still appears if they later enable pacing in Settings.

**Suggestion:** On the goals step, when both toggles are off, show a one-line preview: “Today will show live calorie and step totals without target rings.” If they disable *display* toggles later in Settings, link back: “You can hide sections in Settings → Dashboard.”

**Why it helps:** Sets expectations before the first Today paint and reduces “this isn’t what I configured” support confusion.

---

### 1.3 Vitals+ marketing on screen one competes with the Health value prop

**User story:** First-time user skimming onboarding wants to know what the free app does *today*, not what they might pay for later.

**Current experience:** Page 1 balances “Included with Health access” and a prominent “Try Vitals+ free if eligible” tier. For users who will never subscribe, the premium block is noise before they’ve seen a single real number.

**Suggestion:** Keep Vitals+ mention, but collapse it to a single line + “Learn more in Settings” until after the first successful Today load (or second session). Lead with watch/widget + live totals—the hooks that match App Store promises.

**Why it helps:** Faster time-to-trust; premium story lands better once free value is proven.

---

### 1.4 No onboarding path for “I only care about steps” (or calories)

**User story:** Runner who only wants steps; dieter who only wants calories.

**Current experience:** They must complete goal setup for both metrics or turn toggles off manually later in Settings. Display toggles aren’t on the onboarding flow.

**Suggestion:** Optional third onboarding beat: “What do you want on Today?” with Calories / Steps checkboxes (default both on). Writes `showCalories` / `showSteps` immediately.

**Why it helps:** First paint matches intent; fewer visits to Settings on day one.

## 2. Today (Dashboard) — daily driver

### 2.1 “Pacing” is powerful but opaque without reading Settings

**User story:** User sees “240 cal ahead of usual pace” and wonders *usual compared to what*.

**Current experience:** `PacingPill` shows ahead/behind with no on-dashboard explanation. Full definition lives in Settings footer (same weekday vs all days, lookback window, minimum sample days, “Building pace data: 2/3 days”).

**Suggestion:** Make the pill tappable → lightweight sheet: “Compared to your average at this time on past [Tuesdays / all days] over the last [30 days].” Include progress toward “enough history” when building. Optional: link to Settings pacing section.

**Why it helps:** Users can trust the number without becoming power users; reduces misinterpretation (“why am I behind on Monday?”).

---

### 2.2 Net deficit sign and label don’t match everyday language

**User story:** Someone using Net Deficit to lose weight thinks in “calories left” or “surplus.”

**Current experience:** Positive display (`+500`) means burned more than eaten (deficit); negative means surplus. Footnote helps when food is zero, but the hero number doesn’t say “deficit” vs “surplus” in words—only color and sign.

**Suggestion:** Pair the number with a short word label under it: “deficit” / “surplus” (or user-selectable “remaining budget” style for planners). Keep color coding; add one Settings sentence: “Positive = you burned more than you logged eating.”

**Why it helps:** Aligns UI with how people talk about weight management and avoids dangerous misreads.

---

### 2.3 Enabling Net Deficit from Settings feels like an error when food is empty

**User story:** Pro user enables Net Deficit before logging breakfast.

**Current experience:** Valid state shows footnote about fasting; skeleton then numeric `0 eaten`. Some users read “0 food calories” as broken sync, not “nothing logged yet.”

**Suggestion:** Distinguish three states in copy and iconography: **loading** (skeleton), **no food logged today** (neutral, “Log meals in Apple Health or your food app”), **couldn’t read food** (action: Retry / Settings). Consider a one-time coach mark the first time Net Deficit turns on.

**Why it helps:** Reduces anxiety and unnecessary permission toggling.

---

### 2.4 Drill-down to History is easy to miss

**User story:** User taps the calorie ring expecting detail; or never notices chevrons.

**Current experience:** Calories, steps, and net rows are `NavigationLink`s into focused `HistoryView` with chevrons—good affordance. The ring center doesn’t scream “tap for history”; VoiceOver hints help, but sighted users may not try.

**Suggestion:** Subtle persistent hint on first week: “Tap any metric for charts” under the date header, dismissible. Or a small “History” text chip on the ring footer next to the chevron.

**Why it helps:** Connects Today’s emotional snapshot to History’s analytical view—the main “aha” for engaged users.

---

### 2.5 Goal celebration is invisible except haptics

**User story:** User hits 10,000 steps and feels a buzz but sees no visual reward.

**Current experience:** `GoalCelebration` dedupes haptics per day; no confetti, badge, or transient banner.

**Suggestion:** Lightweight same-day celebration: brief banner or ring flash (“Step goal reached”) once per goal per day. Respect Reduce Motion.

**Why it helps:** Reinforces goals emotionally; makes haptics interpretable.

---

### 2.6 Health notice banners are accurate but stressful on repeat visits

**User story:** User denied Health once; every open shows “Health access is off.”

**Current experience:** Multiple notice types with actionable buttons—good for recovery. No “I know” snooze for users who use the app in counter-only mode with cached data.

**Suggestion:** After user taps “Open Settings” or dismisses twice, collapse to a compact status chip in the header with tap-to-expand. Re-expand automatically if auth state changes.

**Why it helps:** Respects intentional denial without hiding recovery paths.

---

### 2.7 Cached-then-fresh number updates can feel like a glitch

**User story:** User opens app; numbers appear, then change a moment later.

**Current experience:** Cache primes first paint; HealthKit refresh may replace values. Header can show “Updated HH:mm” after success; initial cache paint intentionally skips stale banner.

**Suggestion:** When values change meaningfully (>1% or >50 cal / 100 steps), brief `contentTransition` is already there—add optional subtle “Updated just now” flash or animate delta once. If only cache was shown first, tiny “Syncing…” near the metric that changed.

**Why it helps:** Explains change as freshness, not incorrect data.

## 3. History — understanding the past

### 3.1 Two entry paths to History (tab vs push) blur wayfinding

**User story:** User taps calories on Today, lands in focused History with back chevron; also has History tab in the floating bar.

**Current experience:** Push navigation lives inside `DashboardView`’s `NavigationStack`; tab switches to a separate `HistoryView` instance. Back returns to Today; tab bar stays visible. Works, but mental model is “two Histories.”

**Suggestion:** Either (a) push switches selected tab to History and scrolls/focuses metric, or (b) push uses a clear title “Calories · History” and copy on first use: “You’re viewing history from Today—use the History tab anytime.”

**Why it helps:** Users don’t think they “lost” the tab bar context or duplicate data.

---

### 3.2 Free vs Pro period controls need clearer labeling at tap time

**User story:** Free user taps **Custom** expecting a date picker.

**Current experience:** Paywall opens. 7D/30D/90D/1Y work free (matches positioning). Custom is Pro-only—reasonable, but the segment looks identical to free options.

**Suggestion:** Visual lock on Custom segment (lock icon, slightly muted) for non-Pro; long-press or accessibility hint: “Vitals+ — pick any date range.” Trial users could get one free custom range to taste the feature.

**Why it helps:** Prevents “broken button” perception; turns paywall into informed choice.

---

### 3.3 Chart interaction teaches itself slowly

**User story:** User wants yesterday’s steps without scrubbing blindly.

**Current experience:** Chart selection shows date + value in card header—excellent. Focused metric view adds **Recent Days** list (great for phone detail). Full History tab doesn’t surface the list below every chart.

**Suggestion:** On full History, add collapsed “Recent days” under each chart (or shared list for selected period). One hint: “Touch and drag across the chart” on first visit.

**Why it helps:** Makes History usable for non-chart-literate users and matches watch’s “last 7 days” mental model.

---

### 3.4 Net Deficit empty state doesn’t guide food logging workflow

**User story:** User enables Net Deficit, opens History, sees “No food logged this period.”

**Current experience:** Clear copy; no deep link to Health or named apps (MyFitnessPal, Lose It, etc.).

**Suggestion:** Add actionable line: “Food must be in Apple Health → Sources.” Optional: “Learn how to connect a food app” link to support doc.

**Why it helps:** Closes the loop from empty chart to user action—biggest drop-off for Net Deficit.

---

### 3.5 Deep Trends locked teaser uses synthetic deltas

**User story:** Free user sees blurred +8% / +6% style movement.

**Current experience:** `teaserInsights` fabricates prior period from current averages so the card looks “real.” Ethical for preview, but numeric literals can imply false precision.

**Suggestion:** Blur shape-only rows with labels “Example” or replace numbers with “↑ vs last period” placeholders. CTA: “Unlock real comparison.”

**Why it helps:** Maintains marketing punch without credibility risk when users upgrade and numbers change format.

---

### 3.6 Monthly summary prompt timing

**User story:** Pro user opens History on the 1st; sees “Your monthly summary is ready.”

**Current experience:** Once per calendar month if ≥7 days data—good. Prompt sits above charts; competes with period switching.

**Suggestion:** Tie to natural moment: first History visit in new month, or after month closes (“March is complete”). Allow “Remind me later” / “Don’t show again this month.”

**Why it helps:** Feels like a service notification, not another banner in an already dense scroll.

---

### 3.7 CSV export is responsible but invisible until you know the share icon

**User story:** User wants their data in Excel.

**Current experience:** Export behind share button with strong privacy alert—good. No in-app mention in Settings or History footer for data portability.

**Suggestion:** Settings → Help: “Export history (CSV)” that switches to History and highlights export. Optional export scope note: “Exports visible period.”

**Why it helps:** Surfaces a differentiator (local export) for privacy-conscious users.

## 4. Navigation, tabs, and monetization

### 4.1 Permanent “Upgrade” tab occupies prime navigation

**User story:** Satisfied free user who will never pay.

**Current experience:** Third tab shows lock icon + “Upgrade” forever; Pro shows “Vitals+”. Tab bar never hides—correct for App Store compliance on paywall.

**Suggestion:** For established free users (e.g. 7+ days post-onboarding), demote to Settings-only upgrade entry and use two tabs (Today / History). Or rename tab “Vitals+” with subtler lock and feature preview, not “Upgrade” stigma.

**Why it helps:** Reduces daily nag fatigue; navigation matches actual use (two tabs, 95% of sessions).

---

### 4.2 Trial offer sheet after 5 seconds on Today

**User story:** User opens app post-workout to check calories; sheet slides up.

**Current experience:** Deferred trial pitch (~5s, Today tab only, after onboarding, one-shot flags). History second-touch if launch pitch didn’t run. Thoughtful gating—but still interrupts the core job-to-be-done.

**Suggestion:** Trigger trial on **intent signals**: first PDF tap, first Custom period tap, second session after Deep Trends scroll, not timer on Today. If timer remains, cap at once ever and add “Not now” that snoozes 30 days.

**Why it helps:** Monetization attaches to demonstrated value, not arbitrary delay.

---

### 4.3 Vitals+ features split across three places

**User story:** Subscriber looks for Net Deficit, PDF, Deep Trends.

**Current experience:** Features live in Settings toggles, History (reports, trends), and Vitals+ tab (enable Net Deficit, generate PDF, custom reports). Powerful but fragmented.

**Suggestion:** Vitals+ tab as a **hub**: status + deep links (“Show on Today”, “Open History trends”, “Generate report”) rather than duplicate actions. Single “What’s included” checklist with checkmarks as they enable each piece.

**Why it helps:** Pro users feel they’re getting one product, not scavenger hunt.

---

### 4.4 Embedded paywall has no close button

**User story:** Free user taps Upgrade tab by curiosity.

**Current experience:** `PaywallView(displayCloseButton: false)` on tab 2; user must switch tabs to leave—allowed by design so tab bar stays usable.

**Suggestion:** Short header on paywall: “Switch to Today or History anytime using the bar below.” First visit tooltip pointing at tab bar.

**Why it helps:** Prevents trapped feeling; clarifies that tabs are the exit.

---

## 5. Settings and preferences

### 5.1 Goals save on Done; toggles save instantly

**User story:** User changes calorie goal, toggles pacing, taps outside sheet without Done.

**Current experience:** `onDisappear` applies goal drafts; toggles already persisted. Footer says goals save on Done—mostly true, but easy to miss.

**Suggestion:** Unified feedback: “Saved” toast on Done; if goals edited, show unsaved dot on Done until tapped. Or auto-save goals with debounce like toggles.

**Why it helps:** One mental model for “how settings stick.”

---

### 5.2 Locked Pro toggles that open paywall without explanation

**User story:** Free user flips “Show Net Deficit”; paywall appears.

**Current experience:** Lock icons on toggles; turning on triggers paywall. Toggle snaps back off.

**Suggestion:** On first locked interaction, mini explainer sheet: “Net Deficit needs food calories from Apple Health — included in Vitals+” with preview screenshot, then paywall.

**Why it helps:** Converts curiosity into understanding, not frustration.

---

### 5.3 Coach promo card in Settings (and History)

**User story:** User opened app for self-tracking; sees Elsa / E3 Fitness coaching promo.

**Current experience:** Polished `CoachPromoCard` in Settings and full History scroll—external Safari links.

**Suggestion:** Move to optional “Partner” section collapsed by default, or Settings toggle “Show coaching resources.” Keep support links primary in Help.

**Why it helps:** Protects focus for users who didn’t expect third-party services in a health utility.

---

### 5.4 No “reset onboarding” or guided tour for returning users

**User story:** User skipped goals years ago; wants to re-run setup.

**Current experience:** No explicit re-onboarding; must manually adjust Settings.

**Suggestion:** Settings → “Setup assistant” replay (goals + display toggles + Health check) without clearing data.

**Why it helps:** Supports life changes (new goals, injury, diet phase) without reinstalling.

---

## 6. Widgets and Apple Watch

### 6.1 Widget shows yesterday’s data without strong “open app” CTA

**User story:** Glances at small widget at 7am before phone unlock.

**Current experience:** Falls back to prior day with stale label (“Yesterday”, date)—good honesty. User may think numbers are today’s.

**Suggestion:** Stale state: dim today’s styling, add “Open app to sync” sublabel on medium widget; consider WidgetKit `relevance` for morning reload after common wake time.

**Why it helps:** Widget remains trustworthy at a glance—the primary widget job.

---

### 6.2 Widget doesn’t reflect pacing or “ahead of usual”

**User story:** User relies on widget for motivation during the day.

**Current experience:** Goals and totals; Net Deficit if enabled. No pacing line (reasonable for space).

**Suggestion:** Medium widget optional fourth line: “↑ 180 cal vs usual” when pacing available in cache—or lock screen rectangular only. Requires caching pacing in SwiftData or computing lightweight on timeline refresh.

**Why it helps:** Extends Today’s differentiator to zero-tap surface.

---

### 6.3 Watch: settings only on iPhone

**User story:** User toggles metrics on watch after installing watch app alone.

**Current experience:** “Open Total Calories on your iPhone to turn one on.” WatchConnectivity syncs goals when phone updates.

**Suggestion:** Minimal watch Settings: read-only list of what’s shown + “Change on iPhone” with SF Symbol link graphic. If WatchConnectivity inactive, explain pairing once.

**Why it helps:** Sets expectation on wrist without implying broken sync.

---

### 6.4 Watch calorie breakdown is tap-hidden (same as old phone pattern)

**User story:** User wants active vs resting on watch.

**Current experience:** Tap calories toggles breakdown row—no chevron.

**Suggestion:** Tiny “details” or split icon under calories when breakdown available (Pro + setting synced). Match phone’s active/resting pills naming.

**Why it helps:** Parity across devices for Vitals+ subscribers.

---

### 6.5 Watch trends load failures are quiet

**User story:** User sees “Trends unavailable” under calories.

**Current experience:** Small gray text; no retry.

**Suggestion:** Tap text → retry fetch, or open Help. Explain “Need a few days of Health history.”

**Why it helps:** Wrist users won’t switch to phone to debug silently failed loads.

---

## 7. Accessibility, trust, and copy

### 7.1 Product naming: Total Calories vs Vitals vs Vitals+

**User story:** New user reads App Store “Total Calories”, in-app “Welcome to Total Calories”, settings mention “Vitals+”, bundle `com.jackwallner.vitals`.

**Current experience:** Mostly consistent on customer-facing Today/History; internal brand leaks in premium naming.

**Suggestion:** Pick customer-facing rule: e.g. app name **Total Calories**, premium **Total Calories+** or keep **Vitals+** but define it once in onboarding (“Vitals+ is our premium upgrade”). Use consistently in trial sheet, tab label, Settings header.

**Why it helps:** Reduces “is this the same app?” confusion at purchase time.

---

### 7.2 VoiceOver coverage is good; Dynamic Type on hero numbers may clip

**User story:** Low vision user with large text.

**Current experience:** `Theme.bigNumber` scales with layout; ring and single-metric modes use geometry caps. Very large content sizes might collide with floating tab bar inset.

**Suggestion:** Audit largest accessibility size on SE and Pro Max; allow ring to shrink below 260 cap when `sizeCategory` is accessibility range.

**Why it helps:** Health apps skew older demographics; large type is a core accessibility feature, not edge case.

---

### 7.3 Privacy story is strong but buried

**User story:** Privacy-conscious user wants confirmation before granting Health.

**Current experience:** Help footer in Settings; not on onboarding Health step.

**Suggestion:** One sentence on Health pre-prompt screen: “Read-only. Stays on your device. No account.” Link to privacy policy.

**Why it helps:** Increases grant rate and long-term trust with minimal friction.

## 8. Suggested priority (impact × effort)

### High impact — user feels it daily

1. Pacing explainability (tap pill → sheet)  
2. Net Deficit three-state copy (empty vs error vs loading)  
3. Custom period lock affordance for free users  
4. Trial offer tied to intent, not Today timer  
5. Widget stale-state clarity  

### Medium impact — retention and Pro conversion

6. Vitals+ hub tab vs scattered features  
7. History wayfinding (tab vs push)  
8. Deep Trends teaser honesty  
9. Net Deficit empty → food logging guide  
10. Goal celebration visual feedback  

### Lower effort polish

11. Onboarding preview when goals off  
12. Settings save model unified  
13. Export discoverability in Help  
14. Watch trends retry  
15. Branding consistency pass

## 9. What’s already working well (preserve these)

- **Pull-to-refresh + “Updated HH:mm”** on Today — manual control and freshness visibility.  
- **NavigationLink drill-down** from Today metrics into focused History with **Recent Days** list.  
- **Health notice taxonomy** (access needed / blocked / cached / error) with actionable buttons.  
- **History load error vs stale data** messaging and retry.  
- **Chart scrubbing** with clear selected value header.  
- **Skeleton layout** for Net Deficit food row — avoids layout jump.  
- **CSV export warning** before sharing sensitive health data.  
- **Keeping tab bar visible on paywall** — correct for compliance and escape hatch.  
- **Watch 7-day trend bars** with today highlighted — matches glance use case.  
- **Goal celebration dedupe** — haptics won’t spam on hourly refresh.

## 10. Out of scope for this doc (intentionally)

These are product directions, not UX polish on current flows:

- Social, challenges, leaderboards  
- Cloud sync, accounts, multi-device beyond WatchConnectivity  
- Writing data back to HealthKit  
- AI coaching or meal recommendations  
- Replacing RevenueCat / custom paywall stack
