No account. No ads. No tracking. Your health data stays on your phone, and the app only reads it.

HOW IT WORKS
Total Calories reads active calories, total calories, basal metabolic rate and steps from Health Connect, so it works with any watch, band or app that writes to Health Connect. If you turn on Net Deficit it also reads the nutrition energy you log there. Every permission is read-only and you can change it any time in Health Connect.

When a source only reports part of the picture, the app tells you instead of guessing: days where resting energy is estimated are marked and kept out of your TDEE and BMR averages.

PRIVACY
Your data is processed on your phone. Nothing about your health is uploaded, sold or shared. There is no account and no advertising. Purchases are handled by Google Play.

Total Calories is a personal tracking tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition. Numbers come from the records other apps and devices write to Health Connect and can differ from other apps.
